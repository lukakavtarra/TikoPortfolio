The Gemini CLI Prompt
Role & Objective
Act as a world-class Frontend Developer, UI/UX Designer, and Creative Art Director. Your task is to generate the code and structure for an outstanding, highly interactive, and creative single-page portfolio website for a content creator and videographer named Tinatin Bakuradze.

The Stakes & Context
This portfolio is being submitted to a highly competitive Bank of Georgia contest. The grand prize is a scholarship to the Miami Ad School in Madrid. The design must scream "creative advertising," stand out from standard corporate templates, and perfectly blend Tinatin’s personal flair with her professional videography expertise. It must guarantee a winning first impression.

Visual Concept & UI/UX Design

The Theme: The central metaphor of the site is "serving."

Main Title: The bold, typography-driven hero text must read: "What I am serving in life".

The Canvas: A single-page, full-screen interactive layout with a modern, sleek, and vibrant aesthetic.

The Centerpiece: The background or central focal point must feature a visual of a "plate." In the center of this plate, there will be an image of Tinatin's face.

The Menu (Navigation): Instead of a traditional navbar, arrange interactive icons around the plate (like food or garnishes). These icons represent different facets of her life and career.

Vibe: Modern, edgy, energetic, and highly polished. Use smooth micro-interactions, hover effects, and premium typography.

Interaction Architecture
When a user clicks on the icons orbiting the plate, it should trigger dynamic, well-designed popups, modals, or smooth page transitions. The content categories include:

Videos: A gallery/modal showcasing her videography work with embedded video players.

Links: Important career or project links.

Social Accounts: Stylish links to her active profiles.

Phrases/Quotes: Quick, snappy popups featuring her personal philosophy or creative taglines.

CV/Resume: A clean, scannable modal highlighting her professional experience.

Technical Specifications

Frameworks: Build this using React and Tailwind CSS to ensure a responsive, modern, and easily maintainable structure.

Animations: Utilize Framer Motion (or a similar animation library) to handle the positioning of the icons around the plate, the entrance animations of the modals, and the hover states of the interactive elements.

Responsiveness: The circular "plate" layout must elegantly adapt to mobile screens, perhaps changing from a circular orbit to a vertical, interactive stack on smaller devices.

Code Delivery: Provide modular, well-commented code. Separate the components (e.g., HeroPlate.jsx, IconModal.jsx, VideoGallery.jsx) so it is easy to assemble and test.

Execution Steps for Gemini

Begin by outlining the file structure and the required dependencies.

Provide the code for the main layout, focusing on the complex CSS/Tailwind required to center the plate and distribute the icons in a circular layout around the face.

Create the state management logic (using React hooks) to handle the opening and closing of the various popups/modals.

Provide the code for at least two modal variants (e.g., the Video Gallery and the text-based Phrases popup) to establish the design pattern.

Please generate the necessary code and project structure to bring this creative vision to life.
